Not a massive soccer fan. Caught up with scores on reddit since I'm just a sports fan in general

Watched highlights and caught the random one off games on tv. World Cup watcher prior but not a big follower.

Worldcup in US a massive thing but got bigger the closer to the day. Going to the Old Lansing Ozone's brewhouse for first US game was a great vibe.

Never been to a watch party like that before. The energy was great, game got away from Paraguay quick so time spent on other things.

Talking with friends, eating and just enjoying the moment.

Went to Michigan football games but for some reason those feelings have been lost. 

Watched football games with these folks before but their interest wasn't that much in NFL games. Soccer brought about a different sense of energy in these.

I through that and just my own renewed interest started watching more games. Found a sense of comraderie there. Our conversation's and topics are not aligned usually but this was a great thing to connect with friends again on.
