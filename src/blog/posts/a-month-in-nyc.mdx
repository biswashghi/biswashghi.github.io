I didn't come to NYC with any specific plans outside of a generic thought to "live" more, see more, experience more. Looking back on the month I spent in NYC and writing this up on the flight back I'd say it was a success, and it's already got me itching to come back. From the shores of Coney Island to the hikes around Harriman and the isolation of a weekday Governors Island, I got up to a lot, tired myself out but enjoyed myself.

My first experience in NYC when landing back here for the 4th time was seeing the vibrancy. The people and the energy were explosive, and the dearth of humidity — which had broken about a week before — seemed to add to this frantic energy for people to do even more.

This is the log. Day by day, mostly for my own record.

### Aug 16–22

**Aug 16, Sun**

- **Activities:** Landed. Astoria sublet. Walked to Astoria Park, next to the river, taking it all in.
- **Food:** Taverna Kyclades for dinner. Swordfish, for the first time.
- **Notes:** Talked with a 70 year old man at dinner and got free shots. The swordfish was the same as any other fish.

**Aug 17, Mon**

- **Activities:** Work. Bryant Park movie night — City of God.

**Aug 18, Tue**

- **Activities:** First day at the office. Send-off for a team member who joined our sister team.
- **Food:** The Hall, with her and two others from the team.

**Aug 19, Wed**

- **Activities:** Work.
- **Food:** The Botte for dinner, then Van Leeuwen ice cream.
- **Notes:** The Botte was the worst dinner of the trip. Van Leeuwen became a frequent stop.

**Aug 20, Thu**

- **Activities:** Work and back.

**Aug 21, Fri**

- **Activities:** Dinner with the three coworkers from before and one of their boyfriends.
- **Food:** Angel, on a last minute reservation.
- **Notes:** Good food, good conversation.

**Aug 22, Sat**

- **Activities:** Queens Night Market with a Meetup group I joined randomly for it. Didn't know anyone, had never met them before.
- **Food:** First taste of momo.
- **Notes:** The food was good. The meetup itself was awkward.

### Aug 23–29

**Aug 23, Sun**

- **Activities:** Walked around downtown. Board games with the Meetup group at the Hugh — Werewolf, Coup, Codenames. Then soccer with the coworker's boyfriend's group, where the whole game was a short 5–10 minute sub. Beers after for 30 minutes or so.
- **Food:** The Hugh.
- **Notes:** Sweated through the station. Codenames was my favorite.

**Aug 24, Mon**

- **Activities:** Work. Started the new Insta page.
- **Food:** Two dinners — a chicken sandwich, then cbtm in Jackson Heights.
- **Notes:** cbtm was the best momo of the trip.

**Aug 25, Tue**

- **Activities:** Work, with lunch on the rooftop with colleagues. Then the Federer showmatch at Arthur Ashe — a last minute ticket up in the promenade, a rush home to drop the backpack, and the long ride out to Flushing. Late return.
- **Food:** Honey Deuce.
- **Notes:** It was sad to see the loss of energy for Federer. The qualifying matches outside were much funner than the showmatch.

**Aug 26, Wed**

- **Activities:** Rivercrest trivia.
- **Food:** Darjeeling Kitchen Cafe — the thali.
- **Notes:** The thali was great. Didn't do great at trivia.

**Aug 27, Thu**

- **Food:** First pizza slice of the trip — two, white and chicken bacon ranch, Retro Pizza for lunch. Ibn Hamido for dinner.
- **Notes:** Thought Ibn Hamido would be too much, but the shrimp in garlic butter and the spice rub on the snapper were both amazing.

**Aug 28, Fri**

- **Activities:** Work. Walked around MoMA after dinner, took some pictures, went home.
- **Food:** The Modern.
- **Notes:** Michelin. Unique, but a great experience.

**Aug 29, Sat**

- **Activities:** Late start, rested, built some things — Drift, I think. Circus in Bryant Park. Tried to buy fake purses around Grand Central. Walked around after dinner, then Hungarian House for the dance with the Meetup group and learned some Hungarian steps. Hungarian House itself had organized it.
- **Food:** Muna for dinner.

### Aug 30 – Sep 5

**Aug 30, Sun**

- **Activities:** Kayak in the Hudson around midday, 20 free minutes. Walked to Seaport — waited a couple hours but got 30 minutes of tennis in. US Open activation at Sadis-something around Seaport for a couple hours. Then Chinatown, where a DJ set was happening in the street. Headed home.
- **Food:** A pizza slice during the wait at Seaport. Nom Wah Tea Parlor for dinner.
- **Notes:** Nom Wah was cheap-ish and great.

**Aug 31, Mon**

- **Activities:** Office, a planning meeting. Happy hour with some new colleagues.
- **Food:** Pete's Tavern.

**Sep 1, Tue**

- **Activities:** Office. Walked back to the apartment after dinner.
- **Food:** Darjeeling Kitchen again — pani puri and momo.
- **Notes:** Maybe the second worst food of the trip. Not great.

**Sep 2, Wed**

- **Notes:** Unsure what I did.

**Sep 3, Thu**

- **Activities:** Office. Was going to go to salsa at Pier 72 or 76 and went to a happy hour first to kill time. Nothing was there when I got to the pier, so Bella Abzug Park instead — an hour and a half of a DJ playing old classics. Then Frim Fram for swing lessons.
- **Food:** Two drinks at the happy hour, my first Hugo spritz.
- **Notes:** Didn't know most of the songs, but just vibed and enjoyed the impromptu dance battles.

**Sep 4, Fri**

- **Activities:** Short day, took off early. Astoria to Governors Island for a quiet walk around the island. Back, then the train to Coney Island for the fireworks. Walked the pier and rode the Colossus, then left early.
- **Food:** Radio Kwara for dinner.
- **Notes:** Governors Island was a treat. At Coney Island I got tired and forgot to wait around for the fireworks.

**Sep 5, Sat**

- **Activities:** Late start. Train to the A stop in midtown, then a bike up the Hudson River Greenway — 50 minutes to the Cloisters. Tried L'Industrie for the Monza activation, but it was too busy. Walked through SoHo and the West Village to the pier rooftop, then Little Island for [Thomas Bartlett: Ginsberg at 100](https://littleisland.org/event/allen-ginsberg-at-100/2026-09-05/) with Taylor Mac. Saw Le Bain on the way back. Late back.
- **Food:** Yiwanmen — Chongqing red oil noodles with ground pork.
- **Notes:** The ride was amazing — views of the river, the bridge, all great. The Cloisters had a lot of Christian imagery, not something I have much history with, but the presentation and the architecture made the day. The noodles were so good. Taylor Mac was someone new to me, hidden behind the light in the illustration — unique energy, unique voice. One of the more enjoyable nights of the month. People were talking about Le Bain.

### Sep 6–12

**Sep 6, Sun**

- **Activities:** Late start. Aimed for Tiny Cupboard for comedy and biked through sketchy places for an hour, landing somewhere that didn't feel safe. Dinner instead, then the train to Williamsburg for the Wisconsin/Notre Dame game. Talked with a couple there — a Notre Dame hater and an LSU diehard who met in law school at UT. Left early with the hike in the morning.
- **Food:** Olmo.

**Sep 7, Mon**

- **Activities:** Harriman State Park hike, the entire day, with the Meetup group — the last thing I did with them. Some new people, some familiar faces: Prakhar, Fouad, Nathan, Ranmari, Lisa, Ed. Some went for dinner and a movie after.
- **Food:** Took my own dinner at Nepali Momo Cafe in Jackson Heights.

**Sep 8, Tue**

- **Activities:** Office. Bryant Park for juggling and a co-op poker game, a new one I learned. Then The Stand for comedy — Trevor Wallace and Shane Gillis as surprise acts, with Gianmarco Soresi, Jiaoying Summers, and Mark Normand on the bill.
- **Food:** Korean fried chicken at Turntable Chicken Jazz.
- **Notes:** An amazing, amazing time. My stomach hurt from laughing.

**Sep 9, Wed**

- **Activities:** Don't think I went to the office.
- **Food:** Taverna Kyclades for dinner.

**Sep 10, Thu**

- **Activities:** Office.
- **Food:** Ichiran for dinner.
- **Notes:** Not the best ramen I've had. I've had better.

**Sep 11, Fri**

- **Activities:** Drinks and dinner with the original group of colleagues — all from my team, plus the one who swapped to the sister team. US Open finals right after, then back.
- **Food:** Adore Wine Bar, then The Mala Project.

**Sep 12, Sat**

- **Activities:** The Michigan game at Central Park Tavern. Then the Met, and a lot of time walking there.
- **Food:** Omakase at Sushi by M, UES.
- **Notes:** A funny group of past Ross MBA grads was sitting behind me at the game.

### Sep 13–15

**Sep 13, Sun**

- **Activities:** Sunday morning issue at work, no sleep. Woke up for the Lions game, went back to sleep, and slept again after dinner.
- **Food:** Bhanca Ghar.

**Sep 14, Mon**

- **Activities:** After work, didn't do much.
- **Food:** Lunch at Flaming Grill. Dinner at Retro Pizza, again.

**Sep 15, Tue**

- **Activities:** LGA → ORD → LAN.
